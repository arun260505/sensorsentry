# Hackathon Problem Statements

Captured from the selection portal on 7 September 2026, before the source
screenshots were removed. PS 18 is the one we selected.

## General / product track

**PS 1 — Intelligent Institutional Memory & Decision Traceability Platform**
Build an intelligent platform that connects organizational documents, people,
events and decisions, allowing users to trace what happened, why it happened,
and the evidence behind it.

**PS 2 — AI-Powered Tamil Learning & Grammar Intelligence Assistant**
Develop an AI-powered Tamil assistant that detects grammatical and linguistic
errors, provides contextual corrections, and explains concepts based on the
learner's level.

**PS 3 — Intelligent Reconciliation of Conflicting Digital Records**
Create a platform that identifies duplicate or conflicting records across
different data sources and intelligently reconciles them while preserving
source history and evidence.

**PS 4 — AI-Powered Multi-Channel CRM Assistant**
Build an AI-powered CRM assistant that unifies customer interactions across
calls, emails, messages and other channels while automating records, lead
tracking and follow-up actions.

**PS 5 — Autonomous Operations Intelligence Desk**
Develop an intelligent operations platform that correlates events from multiple
sources, identifies critical incidents, recommends appropriate actions and
maintains an auditable history.

**PS 6 — AI-Powered Learning Management System & Course Builder**
Create an AI-powered LMS that transforms textbooks, PDFs and other educational
material into structured courses, assessments and personalized learning
experiences.

**PS 7 — Decision Simulation & Scenario Intelligence Platform**
Build a decision-support platform where users can modify resources, constraints
and priorities to simulate scenarios, compare outcomes and understand the
resulting trade-offs.

**PS 8 — AI-Powered Gamified & Adaptive Learning Platform**
Develop an AI-powered learning platform that turns academic concepts into games
and dynamically adapts challenges, difficulty and learning paths based on
student performance.

**PS 9 — Dynamic Workflow Intelligence Platform**
Create a platform that tracks cross-department workflows, responsibilities and
dependencies while detecting bottlenecks, delays and changing requirements in
real time.

**PS 10 — Offline AI-Powered Mobile Learning Assistant**
Build an AI learning assistant that works directly on mobile devices with
limited or no internet, providing personalized explanations, Q&A, summaries,
quizzes and revision support.

## Sustainability track

**PS 11 — EcoPack — Sustainable Packaging Optimizer**
Design a software platform that generates packaging solutions while balancing
branding, product protection, sustainability, and cost. It should compare
alternatives, estimate carbon footprint and market cost, and recommend the best
trade-off.

**PS 12 — GreenVest — Land-to-Carbon Investment Planner**
Build a decision-support platform that analyzes land conditions to recommend
suitable plantation strategies, estimate carbon sequestration and potential
carbon credits, and calculate investment returns over time.

**PS 13 — HeatScape — Urban Heat Reduction Planner**
Develop a data-driven platform that identifies urban heat hotspots, determines
their likely causes, and recommends targeted cooling interventions with
estimated impact and implementation cost.

## ARGUS security track

**PS 14 — ARGUS Shield — Adversarial Vision Defense**
Build a defense system that detects and mitigates adversarial images, patches,
or visual manipulation designed to fool ARGUS's vision models.

**PS 15 — PromptGuard — AI Decision Security**
Develop a security layer that identifies and blocks prompt injection,
jailbreaks, and malicious inputs before they can influence ARGUS's critical
decisions.

**PS 16 — ModelVault — Model Extraction Defense**
Create a software protection mechanism that detects suspicious API usage and
prevents attackers from reconstructing or stealing ARGUS's AI models.

**PS 17 — FedSentinel — Federated Learning Defense**
Build a system that analyzes model updates from distributed nodes and detects
poisoned, anomalous, or potentially backdoored contributions.

### ▶ PS 18 — SensorSentry — Spoofing Detection & Sensor Fusion  **[SELECTED]**

> Create an intelligent sensor-fusion system that cross-validates simulated
> sensor data to detect GPS spoofing, false readings, or manipulated
> environmental inputs.

Note the three distinct problems named in that sentence — GPS spoofing,
false readings, and manipulated environmental inputs. Covering all three is
central to our approach.

**PS 19 — ARGUS Ledger — Explainable AI & Audit Trails**
Develop an explainability and logging platform that records ARGUS's autonomous
decisions and provides clear, understandable reasons for critical actions.
